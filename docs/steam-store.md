## Short description (max 300 characters)

Draw empty rooms on a grid and walk through them seconds later. Yellow rooms, still pools, ball
pits nobody has used in years. Explore endless generated levels, hunt for humming VHS tapes – and
when the lights go out in Nightmare, run.

## About This Game

**Liminal Simulator is a sandbox for empty places.** Sketch a floor plan on a grid – one square is
one metre – and watch it appear in 3D as you draw. Then step inside and walk it.

Everything is built for that strange, quiet feeling of a space between places: humming fluorescent
lights, damp carpet, tiled pools that go on too long, a mall after closing time. Textures, lights
and sounds are all generated by the game, so every room you draw looks and sounds like it belongs
there.

**Build**
- Draw walls, floors, water, stairs, escalators, mezzanines, windows and doors
- Raise floors, deepen pools, change ceiling heights, round off corners
- Add water slides, diving boards, ball pits and more than 70 objects
- Generate a room in any theme and make it your own – or press *Make it wrong*

**Explore**
- Ten themes: the yellow rooms of Level 0, the poolrooms, an office, a parking garage, a hotel
  corridor, a basement, a playroom, an empty mall, a school and a hospital ward
- Five moods, from *Normal* to *Nightmare*: dead lights, blackouts, rooms that change behind your
  back – and something that hunts you in the dark
- Doors that lead nowhere – and into the next level

**Game mode**
- Endless generated levels, never the same place twice
- The doors stay locked until you find every VHS tape – listen for the hum
- How deep can you go?

**Keep and share**
- Save your rooms, export them as files, or share them as a link
- Photo mode with an optional VHS look
- Dream walk: let the camera wander through your rooms on its own
