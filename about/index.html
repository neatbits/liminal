About Liminal Simulator

Liminal Simulator lets you draw empty, in-between places – yellow office rooms, still pools, closed malls – on a simple grid, watch them appear in 3D and walk through them in your browser. Textures, lighting and sounds are generated in the browser as you build.

Your rooms and your privacy

  • Rooms you make are saved only in your own browser on your device. Nothing is uploaded, and there are no accounts.
  • A share link contains the whole room, packed into the part of the link after #. That part is never sent to our server – only to the person you send the link to.
  • Photos you take in photo mode are saved straight to your device.
  • The site uses no tracking cookies, and the fonts are served from this site – your browser does not contact Google or any other third party to load them.
  • If advertising or analytics are added later, this page will be updated to describe them.
  • If you send feedback, your message and optional reply address are emailed to hello@liminalsimulator.com. Your room is not sent. A temporary hash of your IP address and submission times are used to limit spam; entries older than one hour are removed when the next message is accepted.

Mood

The mood setting decides how liminal – or how scary – the rooms are while you walk: Normal (just an empty building), Abandoned (dead lights, nobody for years), Liminal (the default: something is subtly wrong, corridors that do not end), Uncanny (darker, more wrong, and something standing at the far end that never comes closer) and Nightmare (constant blackouts, and something hunts you in the dark). Uncanny and Nightmare contain flickering light, sudden darkness and loud noises – choose a lower mood if that bothers you. Your own room in the editor is never changed.
