How it works

1

Sketch in 2D

One square is one metre. Paint floor, walls, water, pillars, lights, doors, stairs and water slides.

2

Watch it in 3D

The 3D view follows every stroke, with baked fluorescent light, reflections and a theme for the mood.

3

Walk through it

Go fullscreen, put on headphones and explore. Open doors, ride the slides, listen to the hum.

4

Share a link

Every room fits in a link. Send it to a friend and they walk exactly the room you made.
